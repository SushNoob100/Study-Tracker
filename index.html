  • Respiration — Glycolysis, Krebs cycle, ETS
  • Organic reactions — SN1/SN2, addition, substitution mechanisms

  • Kinematics + Projectile numericals
  • Electromagnetic induction — Faraday, Lenz applications

  • DNA replication + central dogma + genetic code
  • Mendelian genetics — dihybrid, incomplete dominance

⏱ Topics Students Waste Too Much Time On

Low return for time invested in the CEE context. Limit time here.

  • Analytical chemistry (only 3 Qs — don't over-deep dive)
  • Applied chemistry manufacturing processes (just key facts)
  • Organometallic compounds — only Grignard reactions matter
  • Crystal systems / Bravais lattices (1–2 recall Qs at most)
  • Animal behaviour — reflex/taxis/migration (only 2 Qs)
  • Detailed morphology of Dryopteris/Pinus/Marchantia (just key features)
  • Rotational dynamics — moment of inertia of complex shapes
  • Detailed plant breeding techniques (only 3 Qs Applied Botany)
  • Particle physics / Higgs boson (just conceptual — 1–2 Qs max)
  • Zone refining, electrorefining — just know the name

Designed for an average student · 8–10 hours/day · Goal: Top MBBS Rank

Goal: Complete ALL Priority A topics. Build strong conceptual base before moving to memorization-heavy content.

📝 MCQ Solving Strategy & Negative Marking Management

Critical for MBBS competition — accuracy matters more than attempting all questions

Negative Marking Strategy

  • Attempt ONLY if confidence ≥ 60% → Saves marks from random guessing
  • 4 wrong answers cancel 1 right answer (−0.25 each) — be selective
  • If 2 options are eliminated → attempt. If all options unknown → skip
  • Biology recall Qs → attempt all (you either know or don't)
  • Physics/Chemistry numericals → attempt if formula is known
  • Target: Attempt 165–180 Qs, not all 200

Exam Time Management (3 Hours)

  • First 10 min: Scan entire paper, mark easy Qs
  • Biology (80 Qs) → Target 40 minutes (recall-heavy)
  • Chemistry (50 Qs) → Target 50 minutes
  • Physics (50 Qs) → Target 55 minutes (numericals take time)
  • MAT (20 Qs) → Target 20 minutes
  • Last 15 min: Review marked/skipped questions

Monthly MCQ Targets

  • Month 1: 50–70 MCQs/day (chapter-wise)
  • Month 2: 80–100 MCQs/day (mixed)
  • Month 3: 1 full paper/day (200 Qs timed)
  • Total MCQs solved by exam day: 5,000–7,000
  • Mock Test frequency: Month 1 = 0, Month 2 = 3, Month 3 = 8+
  • Always solve PYQs (Previous Year Questions) alongside chapters
🔬 Subject-Specific Preparation Tips

CEE-specific guidance, not generic NEET advice

🐾 Zoology (40 Qs)

  • Human Physiology (15 Qs): Make organ-system diagrams. Learn cardiac cycle graph. Memorize hormone names + target organs. Know renal filtration values.
  • Plasmodium: Learn life cycle stages precisely — sporozoite→merozoite→gametocyte. Falciparum vs vivax malaria.
  • Earthworm (Pheretima): Segmentation, clitellum position, nephridia types.
  • Frog (Rana): Skin respiration, double circulation, cranial nerves count.
  • Immunology: IgG vs IgM; primary vs secondary response; types of vaccines.

🌿 Botany (40 Qs)

  • Biodiversity (9 Qs): Memorize diagnostic features of each plant group. Floral formulae of Brassicaceae, Solanaceae, Fabaceae, Liliaceae are very frequently tested. Nepal medicinal plants — 5 given in syllabus — learn each one's use.
  • Genetics (6 Qs): Crosses overlap with Zoology genetics — study together. Focus on chromosomal mutations + disorders (Down's, Turner's, Klinefelter's).
  • Photosynthesis: Know exactly which products form in which stage of Calvin cycle. C3 vs C4 plants (difference table).
  • Ecology: Nepal forest types + biogeochemical cycles are frequently asked.

⚗️ Chemistry (50 Qs)

  • Physical Chem (17 Qs): Master numericals in Equilibrium, Kinetics, Electrochemistry, Thermodynamics. Stoichiometry basics must be perfect — they show up as application Qs.
  • Organic (17 Qs): Make a reaction chart for each functional group. Focus: Grignard reagent reactions, SN1 vs SN2 conditions, Aldol condensation, esterification, amine basicity comparison.
  • Inorganic (10 Qs): Know extraction processes briefly (names + stages). Transition metal complex shapes (tetrahedral, octahedral, square planar).
  • Ionic Equilibrium: pH calculation, buffer Henderson equation, Ksp are sure shots.

⚡ Physics (50 Qs)

  • Modern Physics (12 Qs): Highest Physics chapter. Do Bohr radius/energy level formulas. Practice half-life calculations. Know work function problems. Logic gates truth tables — AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR.
  • Mechanics (10 Qs): SHM equations (x=Asinωt etc.), Bernoulli's applications, Stokes law. Newton's laws application problems.
  • Electricity & Magnetism (9 Qs): Kirchhoff's loop/junction method, transformer equations, Faraday's law numericals, AC circuit impedance formula.
  • Waves (8 Qs): Doppler formula for all cases (source moving/observer moving). Young's double slit fringe width formula.

❌ Common Mistakes to Avoid

These mistakes have cost many students their MBBS seat

🔄
Equal Time to All TopicsHuman Physiology (15 Qs) needs 5× more time than Biota & Conservation (2 Qs). Allocate time proportional to weightage.
❓
Guessing Without EliminationWith −0.25 marking, random guessing on 20 unknown Qs loses 5 marks. Always eliminate at least 2 options before attempting.
📖
Reading without MCQ PracticeReading 10 hours without solving MCQs builds false confidence. 40% of study time must be active MCQ solving.
🧪
Skipping Physical Chemistry Numericals17 questions from Physical Chemistry — mostly numerical. Students who only read theory leave these marks on the table.
🔬
Neglecting Modern Physics (12 Qs)This is the single highest-scoring Physics chapter. Students avoid it as "difficult" — but nuclear + semiconductor Qs are actually formulaic.
🌿
Underestimating Biodiversity (9 Qs)Biodiversity is the highest Botany chapter. Students skip it assuming "too much memorization" — but systematic study of plant groups makes these easy points.
📅
No Mock Tests in Month 1Starting mock tests only in Month 3 means wasted weeks. Begin chapter-wise MCQ tests from Day 7 and full mocks from Month 2 Week 1.
🎯
Treating MAT as Unimportant20 questions = 10% of the paper. With CEE's 50th percentile system, MAT can be the differentiator for borderline MBBS ranks. Practice daily.
🗂️
No Error JournalRepeating the same mistakes without tracking them is the biggest preparation inefficiency. Every wrong MCQ must be understood and documented.
⏰
Not Practicing Under Time Pressure3 hours for 200 Qs = 54 seconds/question. Students who don't practice timed conditions often fail to finish the paper despite knowing content.
🇳🇵
Forgetting Nepal-Specific ContentForest types of Nepal, protected areas, Ramsar sites, endemic species — these appear in Ecology, Zoology, Botany. Nepal context questions are almost guaranteed.
📱
Ignoring Biology Revision CyclesBiology memory fades in 7–10 days without revision. Must do at least 3 complete revision rounds of Human Physiology and Genetics over 3 months.
